VSL Dictionary is a fast, offline-first app for browsing the Vietnamese Sign
Language dictionary: 4,300+ signs, video-first and search-first.

FEATURES

- Instant, accent-insensitive search: type "cho" and find "chó". Prefix and
  syllable-aware ranking, with the match highlighted.
- Browse tabs: All (alphabetical, with an A-Z jump rail), Words, Numbers,
  Alphabet.
- Word detail: sign video with 0.5x slow motion, fullscreen playback,
  regional variants (North / Central / South) when available, definitions and
  illustration images.
- English included: English glosses and definitions, and an interface in
  Vietnamese, English or Mixed (English headwords with Vietnamese kept
  underneath). Search works in both languages.
- Favorites and three themes (dark, pure-black AMOLED, light).
- Offline capable: sign videos stream from the CDN by default (small APK) and
  can be downloaded in-app for a fully offline library.

DATA

Dictionary content transcribed from the National Sign Language Dictionary
(qipedc.moet.gov.vn) via the "NgonTay" project. This is a personal-use app,
not the original work.

Source code: https://github.com/ngozz/vsl-dictionary
